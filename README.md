# ✦ De Heptarchia Mystica
### Planetary Day & Hour Clock

> *An interactive Enochian planetary hour calculator based on John Dee's* De Heptarchia Mystica *(Sloane MS 3191)*

---

## Overview

This is a single-file web application that tracks the ruling Heptarchial King, Prince, Minister, and Bonorum Angel of the current planetary hour — in real time, for any timezone on Earth.

*De Heptarchia Mystica* (The Mysticism of the Sevenfold Dominion) is the 16th century Elizabethan Magus John Dee's system of planetary magic structured around the days of the week and the hours of the day. Each day is governed by a Heptarchial King and his Prince. The 24-hour day is divided into six four-hour intervals, each ruled by one of the six Bonorum Angels and one of the 42 Ministers drawn from Dee's received letter-tables.

Practitioners of theurgical and occult sciences can use these planetary hour markers when inscribing talismans, sigils, or lamens — capturing the precise spiritual coordinates of a temporal moment.

### Daily Hour Intervals

Each day is divided into six four-hour intervals beginning at midnight:

| Interval | Hours |
|---|---|
| Minister 1 | 00:00 – 04:00 |
| Minister 2 | 04:00 – 08:00 |
| Minister 3 | 08:00 – 12:00 |
| Minister 4 | 12:00 – 16:00 |
| Minister 5 | 16:00 – 20:00 |
| Minister 6 | 20:00 – 00:00 |

Each interval is governed by one of the six Bonorum Angels of that day and one Minister name drawn from Dee's 42-name letter tables.

## How to Use

### Practical Application

1. Open the clock and select your timezone
2. Click **Enter the Heptarchia**
3. Your current day tab will be selected automatically
4. The glowing crimson card shows the ruling Minister and Bonorum Angel of the present hour
5. Note the King, Prince, Minister, and Bonorum Angel for use in your working

### For Talisman & Sigil Work

Record the active King, Prince, Minister, and Bonorum Angel at the moment of inscription. In occult sciences, capturing the temporal moment — day and planetary hour — anchors a spiritual item to its moment of creation, just as a photograph carries its date. The Heptarchial names may be inscribed directly, or used to inform the timing of the work.

## Installation & Deployment

This tool is a **single self-contained HTML file** with no dependencies, no build step, and no server required. All images and data are embedded inline.

### Run Locally

Simply download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser. No installation needed.

### GitHub Pages

1. Fork or clone this repository
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**
3. Set source to **Deploy from branch → main → / (root)**
4. Your clock will be live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/heptarchia-clock`
